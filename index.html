This First privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

This First is an iPhone app by Juan Pablo Vengoechea Jacome. It helps the person using the phone start one task and close the apps they picked while they start.

What stays on the phone

The task name, its energy cost, the day’s energy, the start length, and whether the task is done stay on the iPhone. The apps the person picks are saved on the iPhone as private tokens. This First does not receive the names of those apps.

Closing and opening those apps uses Apple Screen Time, with the person’s own permission and Face ID. This First does not manage a child’s device.

What is not collected

This First has no account, no ads, and no server for the task or the chosen apps. It does not sell data and it does not send that information to the developer.

Deleting the app deletes what it stored on the phone.
